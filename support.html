Support Us

Paperclips brings together students from across the university, from arts and law to science and engineering, to talk about how AI will shape our society. We'd love your help to keep the conversations going.

Where the money goes

  • Pizza and food for our fortnightly meetups
  • Bigger events beyond our regular meetups, such as policy workshops and AI safety & governance networking events

Get in touch

If you're a faculty, research centre or organisation interested in supporting Paperclips, we'd love to hear from you. Email us at usyd.paperclips@gmail.com.

Supported by

The University of Sydney Union (USU) supports student clubs and societies on campus, including Paperclips.

While supported by the USU, all clubs and societies are independently run and managed. Their activities do not necessarily reflect the position of the USU.

Gigascale Laboratories

Paperclips was started by Vanessa, who is a member of Gigascale Laboratories, an AI safety research collective in Sydney. Gigascale Laboratories has offered compute support if we ever need it. Paperclips is run entirely by students.
