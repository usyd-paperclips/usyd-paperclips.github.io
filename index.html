Paperclips

Paperclips is a society at the University of Sydney. Whether you're a politics, law, philosophy, architecture, CS or maths student (or any other major!), we think you'd really enjoy it.

We have interesting philosophical discussions on what it means to create and to be human in the age of AI. What will be overturned, and what will stay the same?

Questions we could explore

  • AI and democracyDoes AI pose a risk to democracy?
  • Can AI be conscious?Are AIs conscious, and what does that mean for AI safety?
  • Open-source AIIs releasing the code for AI models to the public worth it, given the potential for misuse?
  • Art, writing and mathematics in the age of AIWhat does it mean to make art, to create and to do academic research in the age of AI?

…and more! Our topics change with whatever members are interested in, so suggestions are always welcome.

What's in it for me?

Free pizza and a chance to get to know people from different majors over a shared topic and have deep conversations. If you're an engineer, you'll get free PEP hours too.

We meet fortnightly, location and time TBC.
